# Data

## Experimental data (`data/real/`)

**Source:** Ramsayer J, Kaltz O, Hochberg ME. Data from: Evolutionary rescue in populations of *Pseudomonas fluorescens* across an antibiotic gradient. Dryad Digital Repository; 2012. <https://doi.org/10.5061/dryad.c59q3>. Released under CC0 1.0.

**Associated article:** Ramsayer J, Kaltz O, Hochberg ME. Evol Appl. 2013;6(4):608-16.

`notebooks/02_real_data_Ramsayer.ipynb` reads three analysis-ready tables built from the Dryad files. Section F of the S1 Appendix describes how they were constructed.

| File | Used for | Required columns |
|---|---|---|
| `Ramsayer_PRIMARY_PINN_data.csv` | Training and validation: longitudinal total load at 0, 30, 40, 50 and 100 µg/mL streptomycin | `population_id`, `dose_ug_ml`, `time_h`, `log10_population_plus1`, `resistant_fraction_qR`, `volume_ml`, `status_86` |
| `Ramsayer_qR_48h_EXACT_linked.csv` | 48-h resistant fractions linked to treated rescued populations | `population_id`, `dose_ug_ml`, `time_h`, `resistant_fraction_qR`, `abs_link_error`, `status_86` |
| `Ramsayer_qR_86h_ENDPOINT_unpaired.csv` | Independent 86-h endpoint validation only (Exp 3.10). It is never used for fitting or model selection. | `population_id`, `dose_ug_ml`, `time_h`, `log10_population_plus1`, `resistant_fraction_qR` |

The 86-h endpoint rows cannot be paired with individual treated trajectories, because the endpoint population IDs are not available. They are therefore compared only at the level of their distributions.

## Synthetic data

All synthetic trajectories, sparse/noisy observations and stochastic ensembles are generated by `notebooks/01_synthetic_benchmarks.ipynb` and written to `results/synthetic/data/`.
